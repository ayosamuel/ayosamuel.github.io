Data Solutions Narratives

PAC/FAC Performance Compliance

Solar PV Analytics & Market Intelligence Platform

The Challenge

PAC/FAC testing (€1,000–€100,000+ penalties per 0.1% shortfall) required rigorous performance validation within 2–6 weeks of energization. Manual Excel workflows, fragmented SCADA data, and multi-stakeholder disputes consumed 40+ hours/month per site with zero audit trail.

The Build

Built purpose-built compliance platform: Logic Apps ingest raw telemetry (5–60 min granularity) → ADLS lakehouse bronze/silver/gold → dbt IEC 61724 PR calculations and data quality checks → Power BI PAC/FAC dashboard with GPT-powered contract extraction and pass/fail forecasting.

Impact

Deployment time: 1–2 weeks per site (vs. 6–8 weeks). Bulletproof audit trail eliminated "your spreadsheet vs. mine" disputes. Portfolio managers gained reproducible framework across 100+ sites; post-construction teams saved 40 hours/month on validation.
